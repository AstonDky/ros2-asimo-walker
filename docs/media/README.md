# Demonstration clips / 演示片段

[English README](../../README.md) · [中文 README](../../README.zh-CN.md)

The [10-second montage](asimo-montage.mp4), [GIF preview](asimo-montage.gif) and [still cover](asimo-montage.png) come from the project's [original teleoperation recording](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb173fc7-6ced-482a-a4a4-7628c81fdf40). The simulator view is cropped to a 960 × 800 pixel rectangle at `(1320, 450)` in the source (`crop=960:800:1320:450`). The montage has no audio track.

[10 秒合辑](asimo-montage.mp4)、[GIF 预览](asimo-montage.gif)和[静态封面](asimo-montage.png)均来自项目的[原始遥操作录像](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb173fc7-6ced-482a-a4a4-7628c81fdf40)。画面截取原视频中起点为 `(1320, 450)`、宽高为 960 × 800 像素的仿真区域（`crop=960:800:1320:450`）。合辑没有音轨。

The MP4 is 1328 × 1300 pixels at 30 fps; the GIF is 640 pixels wide at 10 fps. The PNG cover is the montage frame at 3.2 seconds.

MP4 为 1328 × 1300 像素、30 fps，GIF 宽 640 像素、10 fps；PNG 封面取自合辑第 3.2 秒。

The turning and waist panels concatenate the left and right excerpts. All four panels display different moments from the same recording simultaneously; they are not synchronized experiments or a comparison under identical starting conditions. Speed labels refer only to video playback. They do not measure or compare the robot's walking speed, stability or control performance.

转向和扭腰画面分别衔接左右动作片段。四格同时播放的是同一录像的不同时段，不代表同步实验或相同初始条件下的对比。速度标签只表示视频播放倍速，不用于衡量或比较机器人的行走速度、稳定性或控制性能。
